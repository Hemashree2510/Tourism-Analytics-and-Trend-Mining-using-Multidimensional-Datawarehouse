Tourism Country Clustering

Unsupervised Machine Learning: K-Means Clustering on Standardized Arrival Volumes
Unsupervised Learning

Discovers natural groupings among countries based on their international tourist arrival patterns. Following the exact academic pipeline, the model standardizes 2023 international tourist arrival figures using StandardScaler and partitions 49 countries into three distinct clusters using K-Means (random_state=42, n_init=10).

Clustering Methodology & Workflow

Step 1: Data Preparation

Select 49 countries possessing valid 2023 tourist arrivals.

Step 2: Feature Selection

Feature: MostVisited_NumOfArrivals_Millions_2023.

Step 3: Standardization

StandardScale arrivals to zero mean and unit variance.

Step 4: Cluster Assignment

KMeans(n_clusters=3, random_state=42, n_init=10).

Step 5: Statistical Summary

Compute aggregate count, mean, min, and max arrival per cluster.

Step 6: Empirical Interpretation

Interpret clusters from observed statistics rather than assumptions.
