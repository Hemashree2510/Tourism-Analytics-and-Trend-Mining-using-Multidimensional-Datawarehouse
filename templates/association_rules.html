Tourism Association Rule Mining

Discover relationships and transitions between tourist arrival categories using the Apriori Algorithm
Unsupervised Association

Identifies frequent category co-occurrences and directional transition rules across consecutive reporting periods. Transactions are constructed by pairing each destination's 2022 arrival category with its 2023 arrival category across 17 valid countries. The Apriori algorithm detects frequent patterns and derives robust association rules evaluated by Support, Confidence, and Lift.

Apriori Association Mining Workflow

Step 1: Cohort Selection

Select 17 countries having valid arrival values for both 2022 and 2023.

Step 2: Threshold Categorization

Convert numeric values into Low (<20M), Medium (20-50M), High (>50M).

Step 3: Transaction Synthesis

Combine each country's 2022 and 2023 categories into a transaction basket.

Step 4: Boolean Encoding

Encode transaction baskets using mlxtend's TransactionEncoder.

Step 5: Frequent Itemsets

Apply Apriori algorithm with minimum support threshold = 0.10.

Step 6: Rule Generation & Evaluation

Filter rules with min confidence = 0.50 and evaluate Lift.

Evaluation Metrics Guide

Support (s)

The percentage of total transactions in the database that contain both the antecedent and consequent itemsets.
Support(A → B) = P(A ∩ B)

Confidence (c)

The conditional probability that a country belongs to the consequent category given that it was in the antecedent category.
Confidence(A → B) = P(B | A)

Lift (L)

Measures how much more likely the consequent occurs when the antecedent occurs compared to independent chance. Lift > 1 indicates positive association.
Lift(A → B) = P(B | A) / P(B)

*Note: Association indicates statistical co-occurrence and category stability across time; it does not claim direct causality.
