Project Objectives

  1. Multidimensional Warehouse Design: Architect a clean Star Schema comprising a centralized fact_tourism table connected to dim_country, dim_time, dim_tourism_type, and dim_worldbank.
  2. Robust ETL Engineering: Extract raw international tourism figures from Kaggle CSV, clean missing observations without fabricating zeroes, transform data structures, synthesize surrogate keys, and load into the relational warehouse.
  3. OLAP Multidimensional Analysis: Formulate and execute standard On-Line Analytical Processing queries (Roll-Up, Drill-Down, Slice, Dice, Pivot) to view aggregate and drilled-down slices of tourist arrivals.
  4. Supervised Classification: Train a Decision Tree (CART) classifier to categorize destinations into Low (<20M), Medium (20–50M), and High (>50M) tourist arrival tiers based on prior-year volume.
  5. Unsupervised K-Means Clustering: Cluster standardized 2023 international arrival volumes into three natural tiers (Global Mega-Hubs, Major Destinations, and Developing Destinations).
  6. Apriori Association Rule Mining: Identify directional category stability and transition patterns between 2022 and 2023 arrival categories using Support, Confidence, and Lift metrics.
  7. Interactive Academic Web Dashboard: Deliver a full-stack, responsive, and aesthetically refined analytical dashboard for researchers and stakeholders.
